    How to play
    1. Pass the phone around in any order. Tap your name to secretly read your question.
    2. One player got a slightly different question. They don't know it!
    3. Type your answer in secret. Then everyone's answers are shown together, along with the real question.
    4. Vote out loud on whose answer didn't fit, then reveal the odd one out.
